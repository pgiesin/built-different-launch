Step One

Build Your Book Launch Team

Instructions

Start with your people

Every top producer knows that results start with who you knock on first. Your Book Launch Team is the circle of friends, family, clients and teammates who will read an Advance Reader Copy (ARC) and post an honest review on Amazon during launch week.

Ask for honesty, not hype. Real reviews from real readers carry the most weight, and integrity is the Built Different way.

Download the Launch Team Spreadsheet and list everyone you believe will show up for you. Start with the people who believed in you before you believed in yourself.

Important reminder

Reviewers need an active Amazon account with at least $50 spent on Amazon in the last 12 months. Without that, Amazon won’t accept their review.

Downloads

Your Advance Reader Copy of Built Different is ready for you and your reviewers.

This is a private, copyrighted edition for your launch team only. Please don’t share it beyond them before December 4, and treat your fellow authors’ stories with the same respect you’d want for your own.

Then find a quiet spot and read it cover to cover. You’ll see yourself in more chapters than your own.

Make the ask

You have your list. Now email each person: tell them you’re about to become a published author and invite them onto your launch team. The template lays out exactly what you’re asking and when.

Subject: Would you do me a favor, [FIRST NAME]?

Dear [FIRST NAME],

I have some exciting news: I’m a contributing author in Built Different: How to Serve and Succeed in Sales, a new book featuring some of the top performers in door-to-door sales. It launches on December 4, 2026.

When I thought about who I’d trust with an early copy, you were one of the first people who came to mind. I’m putting together a small launch team to read the Advance Reader Copy (ARC) before it’s published and post an honest review on Amazon during launch week.

Here’s what that looks like:

  • I’ll email you a PDF of the book in the next few days.
  • You’ll have about two weeks to read it and write your review.
  • You’ll hold your review until I send instructions, then post it on Amazon between December 4 and 8.

Would you join my team? Just reply in the next few days and I’ll send your copy as soon as I hear back.

Your support means more than you know. Thank you for being one of the people in my corner.

Step Four

Launch Team Check-In

Instructions

Follow-up is where most deals are won, and it’s the step most people skip. Don’t. About a week before launch, send a friendly check-in to everyone who received the ARC.

Send it to every team member, even those who have already finished. It reminds them that December 4 is close and that you’re counting on them.
